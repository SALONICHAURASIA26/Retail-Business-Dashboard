# Retail Business Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze retail sales performance across products, customers, and regions — helping stakeholders track KPIs and make data-driven decisions.

## 📊 Overview

This project simulates a real-world retail business intelligence use case. It consolidates sales, product, and customer data into a single interactive dashboard with **4 report pages**, enabling quick insights into revenue trends, top-performing categories, and regional performance.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data but often lack a centralized view to track performance. This dashboard was built to give business teams (executives, product managers, and marketing) a single source of truth to:
- Monitor overall sales and profit performance
- Identify top-performing products, brands, and categories
- Understand customer and regional trends
- Support data-backed decisions on inventory, marketing, and pricing

## 🗂️ Dashboard Pages

1. **Executive Dashboard** – High-level KPIs (Total Sales, Profit, Profit %, Total Orders), monthly sales trend, sales by country (map), and profit/sales by category.
2. **Product Analytics** – Product-level performance: total products, categories, sales by brand (treemap), and top-selling products.
3. **Customer Analytics** – Customer segmentation and purchase behavior insights.
4. **Insights & Recommendations** – Key business insights derived from the data, paired with actionable recommendations for stakeholders.

## 📈 Key Insights

- Total Sales reached **55.76M** with a **58.58%** profit margin
- **Home Appliances** generated the highest sales among categories
- **Adventure Works** emerged as the top-performing brand
- **USA** contributed the highest revenue by country
- Sales dipped in **April** and peaked in **February & December** (seasonal trend)
- Top 10 customers contributed a significant share of total revenue

## 💡 Recommendations

- Increase inventory ahead of festive/peak months
- Focus marketing efforts on Home Appliances
- Promote low-selling products through targeted discounts
- Expand top-selling brands into new regions
- Introduce loyalty offers for high-value customers
- Optimize pricing strategy for low-performing categories

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** – Data modeling, DAX measures, report design
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Calculated measures (Total Sales, Profit, Profit %, etc.)
